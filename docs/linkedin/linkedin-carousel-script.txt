Power Automate Consumption Analyzer

Format
- LinkedIn document post
- Export as PDF
- 5 slides
- Keep each slide visually clean: one title, 2-4 short bullets, one screenshot where relevant

Slide 1 - Problem
Title:
Power Automate licensing is not always easy to understand

Body:
- I have had many customer conversations around Power Platform and Power Automate licensing.
- The rules exist, but applying them in practice is not always straightforward.
- Especially when you need to explain when Premium is enough and when Process is really needed.

Footer:
I built a small tool to help make that easier.

Visual:
- No screenshot required, or use a blurred/cropped product image as background if preferred.

Slide 2 - Upload and start
Title:
Start with your PPAC export

Body:
- Upload CSV or Excel exports from the Power Platform Admin Center.
- Supports Licensed User, Non-Licensed User, and Per Flow reports.
- Drag and drop the file and start the analysis.

Visual:
- Use: docs/screenshots/main screen 1.jpeg

Slide 3 - User overview matrix
Title:
See who is compliant and what is recommended

Body:
- The overview matrix shows who is covered and who is not.
- It highlights whether Premium is enough or whether Process licensing is recommended.
- The matrix also acts as a filter for the detailed list below.

Visual:
- Use: docs/screenshots/User Details 1.jpg

Slide 4 - Drill into a specific user
Title:
Go deeper with usage over time and by environment

Body:
- Open a user to see trends over time and detailed environment usage.
- This matters because Process licensing cannot cover multiple environments.
- The detailed breakdown helps identify where the actual issue sits.

Visual:
- Use: docs/screenshots/User Details 2.jpg

Slide 5 - Environment view, pricing, and CTA
Title:
Review by environment and adapt the pricing model

Body:
- Switch to environment-level analysis to understand where risk is concentrated.
- Adjust Premium and Process prices to estimate impact in your own context.
- I published this as source code and managed solution.

CTA:
Would this be useful for you or your customers? What would you add?
